Questions

What people ask first.

Do we need technical staff?

No. That's the point. We build it, host it and look after it. You need someone who knows how the job is done today and can tell us when the system gets it wrong.

How long does a first project take?

Most first projects are small on purpose, so a working version is usually a matter of weeks. We'll give you a date in the plan before you commit.

What does it cost?

It depends on the job, so we price each project in the written plan: a build cost and a monthly running cost, set against what it should save or earn. If the sums don't work, we'll tell you.

Is our data safe?

We use established business-grade AI providers that don't train on your data, limit access to what the system needs and agree in writing where your data is held.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

It will, sometimes. So we design for it: uncertain cases go to a person, important actions need approval, and everything is logged so mistakes can be traced and fixed.

Will it replace our staff?

The projects we take on remove the repetitive parts of jobs. What you do with the time saved is your decision.
